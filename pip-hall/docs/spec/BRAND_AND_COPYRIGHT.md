# PIXEL PASS — Brand & Inspiration Guardrails

## Goal

The product should intentionally evoke the feeling of a classic Nintendo-era platform game while remaining a distinct original project.

## Allowed Direction

Use general visual concepts such as:
- pixel art
- side-scrolling game composition
- floating blocks
- coins
- stars
- pipes
- mushrooms as a generic visual idea
- world maps
- retro UI
- arcade sound-like interaction patterns created from original/free assets
- chunky buttons
- monochrome console aesthetic

## Do Not Use

Do not ship:
- Nintendo logo
- Mario logo
- official Mario character sprites
- official Nintendo UI assets
- official Nintendo sounds
- official game music
- copied screenshots
- Nintendo product names as branding
- misleading language implying official affiliation

## Brand

Use:

**PIXENDO** — fictional retro-game platform

**PIXEL PASS** — member-card system

Suggested PIXENDO supporting phrase:

**Your people. Your projects. One playable world.**

Alternative:

**Collect the people. Discover the work.**

## Tone

- playful
- nostalgic
- technical
- confident
- concise
- not childish

## Avoid

Do not use copy such as:
- "Official Nintendo Member Cards"
- "Super Mario Organization"
- "Nintendo Portfolio"
- "Mario ID"

The app should feel inspired by the genre and era, not impersonate the company.
